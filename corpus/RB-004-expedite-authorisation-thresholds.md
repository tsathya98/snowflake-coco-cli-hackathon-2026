# RB-004 — Expedite authorisation thresholds

## 1. Cost threshold

Expedited freight may be arranged automatically where the incremental cost is under two thousand
units of currency and the affected SKU is not on quality hold.

Above that threshold, or where the shipment is inbound to a regulated production line, a human
approver must authorise the spend.

## 2. Justification

Record the justification against the shipment. A justification that restates the trigger is not
a justification; it must state what was checked and why expediting was the proportionate
response.

## 3. Reversibility

An action's undo path is part of its risk, not an afterthought.

Where a proposed action has no stated means of reversal, treat the absence as grounds for more
scrutiny, not less, and route it for human approval regardless of cost. An approver must be
shown the undo path, or shown explicitly that there is none, before deciding.

## 4. Interaction with quality holds

No expedite may be arranged against material under quality hold, at any cost, and regardless of
cover. Quality disposition precedes logistics.
